MEASUREMENT GUIDE

Measure consistently.

Use the same tape position every time. Consistency matters more than a single reading.

☀
Best time to measure

Morning, after using the toilet, before breakfast, wearing light clothing. Stand relaxed, exhale normally, and measure twice.

How the app calculates values

BMI

Weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Does not directly distinguish fat from muscle.

Waist-to-height

Waist circumference ÷ height. Below 0.5 is a common screening goal.

Waist-to-hip

Waist ÷ hip. Above 0.90 in men is a commonly used central-fat screening threshold; not a diagnosis.

Male Navy body-fat estimate

Uses neck, navel circumference and height. It may be wrong by several percentage points. It does not measure visceral fat or muscle mass directly.

BodyTape is a self-tracking tool, not a diagnosis or treatment. Because you have reported exercise-related palpitations, get medical guidance before significantly increasing exercise intensity.
